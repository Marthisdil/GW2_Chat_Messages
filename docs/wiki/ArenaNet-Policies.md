# ArenaNet's policies

GW2 Chat Messages is a convenience tool: it types chat messages that **you** start, with a key
press or a click, into the channels you chose. It doesn't play the game for you, and it must
never be used to.

## The policies that apply

- **[Policy: Macros and Macro Use](https://help.guildwars2.com/hc/en-us/articles/360013762153-Policy-Macros-and-Macro-Use)**
  (ArenaNet support site). In short: macros are allowed while you're at the keyboard, as long as
  they don't exploit the game or give you an unfair advantage over other players. Macros that
  run while you're away are never allowed. The page lists what isn't allowed, for example
  automating skills, skill chains in competitive play, farming, using macros to take part in
  activities or events, macros on more than one account at a time, and "bots". Each macro should
  be a single action that needs you to start it again.
- **[Policy: Third-Party Programs](https://help.guildwars2.com/hc/en-us/articles/360013625034-Policy-Third-Party-Programs)**.
  ArenaNet doesn't allow programs that give an unfair advantage, automate actions in the game or
  let someone play while away. It doesn't review, approve or endorse any third-party program, and
  you use one at your own risk.

Read the pages themselves: they're ArenaNet's words, and they can change. The summary above is
only a guide.

The addon's options page (Nexus > Options, the Chat Messages page) has a link to the macro
policy under the version number and build.

## How the addon fits

- **You start every send.** A macro is sent only when you press its hotkey, pick it in the quick
  menu or click **Send now**. Nothing is sent on a timer, on a game event, or while you're away.
- **One press, one macro.** A press sends that macro's lines once, then stops. Presses that come
  while a macro is still being sent are ignored, never queued.
- **Chat only.** The addon opens chat, types the text and presses Enter. It doesn't use skills,
  move your character, target, loot or press any other game key.
- **Only where you play.** It only ever sends to the game window you're using, on your account.

## Don't use it to

- automate gameplay of any kind, or anything that should be a skill, a movement or an action;
- send messages while you're away from the keyboard (AFK), or keep sending to keep yourself
  "active";
- spam chat, flood a channel or harass other players (ArenaNet's
  [Code of Conduct](https://www.arena.net/en/legal/code-of-conduct) applies to everything you
  send);
- take part in events or activities that expect a player's own input.
